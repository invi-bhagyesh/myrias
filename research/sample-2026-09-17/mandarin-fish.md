## Affected animals

## Evidence and interpretation

**Measured results and uncertainty:**

**Study design, experimental unit, attrition, independence, funding:**  
Randomized controlled trial with 12 tanks as experimental units. No attrition reported. Independence of tanks assumed. Funding sources not disclosed.
